Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert time between two time zones?

Choose a From and To time zone, enter a date and time, then select Convert. You'll see the equivalent local time in the target zone along with the exact hour difference.

Does it account for Daylight Saving Time?

Yes. Every conversion and world clock uses the IANA time zone database built into your browser, so Daylight Saving Time (DST) shifts are applied automatically for the date you pick.

How is my local time zone detected?

Your device's time zone is detected automatically and shown as a live clock with its current UTC offset. No location permission or sign-in is required.
